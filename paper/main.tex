\begin{abstract}
Dyadic motion descriptions can share coarse interaction semantics while differing in synchronization and relative timing. We study a deliberately narrow question: whether fixed, motion-derived complex relation evidence adds repeatable bidirectional retrieval utility to a qualified interaction-aware motion--language encoder. PhasePair pools six fixed Morlet bands over paired kinematic activity, forms ordered relation tokens, and adds a score-symmetric residual to a frozen MIME-style base. The registered comparison includes capacity-, marginal-frequency-, mean/difference-DCT-, relation-, and phase-family controls under matched residual-head capacity. This document specifies the method and sealed evaluation plan only. All empirical cells remain held because rights, assets, CLIP/runtime resolution, optimization, training, and test authority are absent.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Two-person actions such as coordinated exercise, dancing, or sparring can be textually similar even when their temporal relationship differs. A global interaction embedding may therefore align the action category while underrepresenting whether the actors move together, oppose one another, or exhibit a consistent relative phase. Existing work already occupies broad claims about interaction-aware motion--language encoding, frequency representations, and cross-wavelet analysis \cite{mime2026,interedit2026_hold,wamo2025_hold,grinsted2004,toiviainen2021}. Our claim boundary is consequently narrow: PhasePair is not a new motion backbone, not local cross-wavelet coherence, not causal lead--lag inference, and not an official reproduction of any baseline.

We register one falsifiable contribution: a six-token, fixed-kinematic complex-relation residual whose final motion--text score is invariant to actor order by construction. The residual must improve over a strength-qualified in-house MIME adaptation and five matched controls. Until the complete experiment and authority chain closes, this contribution remains a hypothesis rather than a result.

\section{Method}
\subsection{Qualified base and score-symmetric residual}
Under the registered method, the audited common adapter would produce ordered AB and BA motion passes for each actor pair. A future paper-audited, full-gallery-strength-qualified in-house MIME adaptation would supply frozen ordered motion embeddings and text embeddings; no such qualified runtime artifact exists at this draft state. It would not be described as an official MIME reproduction. Let the ordered global scores be dot products between normalized motion and text embeddings, and let an ordered residual head score six relation tokens. The only permitted final scoring rule is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:score}
\begin{aligned}
G_{ij}&=\tfrac12\!\left(\langle g_i^{AB},t_j\rangle+\langle g_i^{BA},t_j\rangle\right),\\
R_{ij}&=\tfrac12\!\left(r_{ij}^{AB}+r_{ij}^{BA}\right),\\
S_{ij}&=\exp\!\bigl(\operatorname{clamp}(\ell_{\mathrm{MIME}},\log 1,\log 100)\bigr)
          \left(G_{ij}+0.2R_{ij}\right).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The AB/BA average makes the score actor-swap invariant; it does not assert encoder equivariance. If all active relation masks are zero, the residual is defined as exactly zero and the score falls back to the same frozen base.

\subsection{Six-band relation tokens}
Five fixed body-group activity channels would be computed from pre-normalization kinematic positions and velocities. Six fixed complex Morlet sliding-window dots use center frequencies 0.75, 1.125, 1.6875, 2.53125, 3.796875, and 5.6953125 Hz, with three-cycle lengths 120, 80, 54, 36, 24, and 16 frames at 30 Hz. No padding, kernel reversal, or kernel conjugation is allowed. Within each band, same-name body groups would be pooled into two auto spectra and one cross spectrum; the latter defines a coherence-like magnitude, principal relative phase, and a wrapped phase-delay proxy. These are motion-derived descriptors, not physical or causal measurements.

For band index $k\in\{1,\ldots,6\}$, the ordered 13-dimensional token and its fixed fields are
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:token}
\begin{aligned}
S_{AB,k}&=\operatorname*{mean}_{c,t}\overline{U_{A,k,c}[t]}U_{B,k,c}[t],\\
\gamma_k^2&=\frac{|S_{AB,k}|^2}{(S_{AA,k}+10^{-12})(S_{BB,k}+10^{-12})},\\
\phi_k&=\operatorname{Arg}(S_{AB,k}),\qquad
\delta_k=-\frac{\phi_k}{2\pi f_k},\\
\mathbf r_{AB,k}&=\bigl[\log(S_{AA,k}+10^{-12}),\\[-2pt]
&\qquad \log(S_{BB,k}+10^{-12}),\gamma_k^2,\\[-2pt]
&\qquad \cos\phi_k,\sin\phi_k,2f_k\delta_k,v_k,\\[-2pt]
&\qquad \operatorname{onehot}_6(k)\bigr]\in\mathbb R^{13}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Here $v_k$ is the signed-phase-valid bit. Swapping actors exchanges the auto spectra, preserves $\gamma_k^2$ and cosine, and changes the signs of sine and wrapped delay. Each token passes through the shared $13\!\rightarrow\!256\!\rightarrow\!512$ Linear--GELU--Dropout--Linear projector. Ordered projected-token similarities $d_{ijk}$ are pooled by the registered mask-aware rule $a_{ijk}=m_{ik}\exp(4d_{ijk})/\sum_l m_{il}\exp(4d_{ijl})$ and $r_{ij}^{\mathrm{ord}}=\sum_k a_{ijk}d_{ijk}$, with an exact zero residual when all slots are masked. Only the aggregated $R_{ij}$ in Eq.~\ref{eq:score} receives the fixed score-level coefficient 0.2; there is no learned filter, auxiliary phase loss, or delay loss.

\subsection{Training objective}
Batches are formed by motion/source cluster, and every official caption associated with the same motion cluster is a positive. The frozen base and each residual head share the same positive mask. The sole residual-training objective is symmetric multi-positive InfoNCE:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:loss}
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_{m2t}&=-\frac1I\sum_i\left[\operatorname{LSE}_{j:P_{ij}=1}S_{ij}-\operatorname{LSE}_{j}S_{ij}\right],\\
\mathcal L_{t2m}&=-\frac1J\sum_j\left[\operatorname{LSE}_{i:P_{ij}=1}S_{ij}-\operatorname{LSE}_{i}S_{ij}\right],\\
\mathcal L&=\tfrac12\left(\mathcal L_{m2t}+\mathcal L_{t2m}\right).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Masked, max-shifted log-sum-exp is required. There is no swap or known-delay auxiliary objective; swap symmetry follows from Eq.~\ref{eq:score}, while known-delay behavior is only a data-free numerical diagnostic.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
If every registered gate passes, the supported conclusion would remain conditional and narrow: fixed dyadic complex-relation evidence provided incremental, direction-free retrieval utility on the exact frozen gallery and protocol. It would not establish a broad first, causal timing, global lead--lag, local cross-wavelet coherence, encoder equivariance, or generalization beyond the frozen study. A generic-token win would suggest capacity rather than relation binding; failure against a relation control would reject the relation thesis; failure against phase stripping would reject the phase-family thesis. Any failed rights, integrity, runtime, optimizer, dropout, data-lineage, energy-floor, or statistical-assumption gate stops the claim.

The fixed bands, pooled same-body-group statistic, principal wrapped delay, and source-cluster bootstrap define further limitations. The descriptor does not model all body-part cross-pairs, does not unwrap phase across bands, and does not infer actor roles from captions. Three seeds quantify only the registered training repetition; they do not define a seed population. The conditional bootstrap target is the exact frozen gallery and depends on its registered cluster assumptions.
